Job Title: HR Recruiter

We are looking for an HR Recruiter to join our Human Resources team.

Requirements:
- 0 to 2 years of experience in recruitment.
- Knowledge of candidate sourcing and resume screening.
- Experience coordinating interviews.
- Good communication skills.
- A Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field.

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with LinkedIn Recruiter.
- Familiarity with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
- Knowledge of Microsoft Excel.

Responsibilities:
- Source candidates through job portals and professional networks.
- Screen resumes against job requirements.
- Coordinate interviews with candidates and hiring managers.
- Maintain recruitment records and hiring reports.
- Support offer management and onboarding.
